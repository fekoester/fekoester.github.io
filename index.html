Two lines of research

I · LLM training dynamics

Training as an action principle.

Forward pass, backprop and gradient flow are the Euler–Lagrange equations of one action. Data enters as a source and the optimizer metric as the kinetic term. That explains why data reweighting is zero-sum and why only the metric changes the learning path.

II · Reservoir computing

Fix the dynamics. Learn the readout.

How much can a fixed, untrained dynamical system compute? With a state-dependent attention readout, a lot: better chaotic forecasting from smaller reservoirs, several attractors in one model, and language models trained without a single gradient through the reservoir.
