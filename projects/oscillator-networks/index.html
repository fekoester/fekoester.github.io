A header-only C++17 integrator for coupled phase oscillators near a saddle-node-on-invariant-circle bifurcation, with delayed feedback control. Original code from 2017, rewritten in 2026.

Runs live in your browser

Turn up the coupling until they lock

Fifty active rotators with natural parameters bi spread around 2, all coupled to all (normalised by degree). At ε = 0 each runs at its own frequency. Raise ε and they pull each other into a common rhythm. Push the spread of b below 1 and some oscillators fall below the SNIPER bifurcation: they stop, excitable, until the others drag them along.

order parameter R = –colour: natural frequency, slow (blue) to fast (amber)

RK4 with step 0.01, the repository’s right-hand side without the delayed-feedback term. Even incoherent SNIPER networks have R ≈ 0.3, because the oscillators linger near φ = 0.
